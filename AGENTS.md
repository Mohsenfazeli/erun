## Website architecture
- The homepage stays a single snap-scrolling page in the index route with section anchors (home, expertise, approach, applications, about, lab, contact).
- Inner pages are their own routes wrapped in `PageShell` (`src/components/site-chrome.tsx`), which reuses the homepage header and footer and ends with the consultation call to action. Their navigation links back to homepage sections with `/#id`.
- Positioning: a specialized consulting firm at the intersection of economics and technology. Economics defines the positioning without dominating the vocabulary. Hero headline: "Turning Complexity into Better Decisions." (`HEADLINE`/`TAGLINE` in `src/lib/site.ts`, edit it only there); do not add competing slogans. Brand name is always "Erun Consulting Group"; based in Victoria, BC. No decorative numbering or tiny uppercase micro-labels; all text must reach 4.5:1 contrast. The hero visual is a canvas dot wave (`src/components/particle-wave.tsx`), with hero buttons "Explore Our Services" and "Learn About Erun". Every "Book a consultation" goes to /book (Cal.com, `VITE_BOOKING_URL`); the secondary CTA is "Send a message" (/#contact). Privacy policy at /privacy must match what the code does. Capabilities: Analytics, Automation and AI Adoption (`SERVICE_NAMES`); client value: deeper insights, efficient workflows and better decisions.
- Service pages live at `/services/<slug>` (economic-analytics, economic-workflow-automation, ai-adoption-in-economics); their copy is in `src/lib/services.ts` and they share `ServicePage`. Earlier slugs (data-analytics, automation, ai-implementation, analytical-automation, ai-adoption) are 301 redirects straight to the current pages; keep them. Keep claims general: no client names, statistics or results that are not verified.
- "Book a consultation" always leads to the homepage contact form (`/#contact`); there is no calendar booking.
- The hero visual is `EconomicFlow` (`src/components/economic-flow.tsx`): SVG curves drawn in CSS pixels with requestAnimationFrame, coloured from `--primary` via CSS classes. Keep it light: 30 fps, paused off screen and in hidden tabs, one still frame for reduced motion, and a seamless loop (whole-number speed multiples of the loop phase).
- Contact enquiries go through the `sendEnquiry` server function (Resend API, key in `RESEND_API_KEY`); never imply an unsent enquiry was delivered, and never commit secrets.
- The Lab is a homepage section (`#lab`, after About; `/lab` redirects there) with prototypes at `/lab/<slug>`; copy and slugs are in `src/lib/lab/products.ts`, and prototype pages share `LabProductPage`. All Lab data is sample data for a fictional company: label it as such and never present it as real figures.
- The Lab HR Assistant goes through the `askLabAssistant` server function; Gemini is called from `src/lib/lab/assistant.server.ts` with `GEMINI_API_KEY`, and it must keep working offline (handbook quotes) when the key is missing or the call fails.
